With a game chosen, a press switches the Footstep Booster to that game and turns it on; a second press turns it off. Without a game the key only toggles on/off.

The Footstep Booster runs inside JBL QuantumENGINE on this PC, so QuantumENGINE must be installed and its service running. The QuantumENGINE window shows changes made here only after it is reopened.
